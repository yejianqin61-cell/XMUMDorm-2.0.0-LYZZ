XMUMDorm brings campus information, student conversation, and everyday services together for the Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM) community.

## Main Features

### Campus Square

- Notices from school, college, and major organizations
- Campus trends, clubs, freshman guides, and useful campus information
- A controlled banner space for campus activities and partner promotions

### Tree Hole

- Create posts with images, comment, like, and save
- Browse conversations by topic
- Report inappropriate content to help keep the community welcoming

### Canteen

- Explore canteen areas, shops, and dishes
- See popular dishes, shop rankings, and recent reviews
- Add useful food information, photos, and reviews

### Clubs and Student Help

- Discover clubs, follow updates, publish activities, and register for events
- Browse and publish second-hand listings
- Post, take, and track campus errand requests

### My Zone

- Manage your profile and visibility preferences
- Keep a schedule, to-do list, and diary
- Read announcements, interactions, and service notifications in the mailbox

## Use XMUMDorm
